A Piece of Whole is an 8-week somatic wellness and civic engagement program, delivered as a guided group experience through a beautifully crafted iOS app.

Each week layers self-regulation practices with co-regulation, community building, civic agency, and civic engagement — grounded in the belief that personal wellbeing and collective action are inseparable.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Daily Check-In — privately track your body, mood, stress, and capacity each day
• Practice Library — somatic exercises organized by week and theme
• Civic Lessons — accessible, non-partisan civic education modules
• Circle — share reflections and support your cohort peers
• Journal — private space for weekly integration
• My Piece — articulate your values, gifts, and current contribution

HOW IT WORKS

You join a cohort — a small, facilitated group moving through the 8-week curriculum together. Your check-ins and journal are private. Your circle shares are visible only to your cohort. Facilitators guide the experience without seeing your personal data.

THE PHILOSOPHY

Trauma-informed. Somatic first. Civically rooted. A Piece of Whole is designed for people who sense that caring for themselves and showing up for their community are the same act.

PRIVACY

Your check-ins and journal entries are private — only you can see them. Circle posts are shared within your cohort only. We do not sell your data.
